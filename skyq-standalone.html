Operations Manual

FORM S-27 · REV. 12
1

Each morning the Bureau files one secret flight route — an origin and destination pair. Identify both airports in 5 filings. Every player worldwide gets the same route.

2

File a guess with the airport search. Each wrong airport reports its great-circle distance to the hidden airport on that side. Miss by 300 km? You're warm. Miss by 9,000? Rethink the continent.

3

Every filing declassifies one more line of the manifest: duration → aircraft → type → direction → airline. The route chart is stamped only when the case closes.

4

Stuck? A HINT reveals one airport — earn hints by watching a sponsor notice, and each one costs 150 points. A first-filing solve pays 1000.

5

Three classes of service daily — Easy, Medium, Hard. Solve all three to keep the streak postcard on your wall.
